\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The theory of vehicle currencies rests on a state its usual data cannot contain. If an incumbent is cheap to trade because it is used and used because it is cheap \citep{Krugman1980VehicleCurrencies,KiyotakiWright1989MoneyMedium}, then observing the incumbent in use says nothing about how much of its position the cost advantage accounts for, and the informative moments, when an incumbent occupies the role while a cheaper path is open, are exactly the moments whose counterfactual is missing from exchange-rate records. This paper takes the question to a currency system where the counterfactual is computable and finds those moments common: on the days priced for it, more than a quarter of realised intermediated routing would have done better going direct at the state it executed in.

The frequency is worth having, and the duration question is what the setting opens. Leadership on a pair changing hands is one event, and conditioning it on whether the leading asset was beaten on cost gives a comparison that is symmetric by construction, because both conditions are read from the same pairs on the same days with the same pricing engine. Measured that way over a sixty-day window at block-level state, losing the cost advantage raises the daily chance of losing the role by roughly a sixth. That is a small response for a market in which route selection is recomputed at every quote and no trader carries a relationship, and it is the incumbent-protection case, where the competitive one would have the role tracking cost closely.

Three descriptive results stand independent of that estimand. The vehicle role migrated from the platform's native asset to a stable numeraire inside six years, which places a dollar-denominated claim at the centre of a market that opened around its own unit \citep{CataliniDeGortariShah2022Stablecoins,LyonsViswanathNatraj2023Stablecoins}. The role fragmented over the same period, with the effective number of vehicles rising and the leader's share falling on the value basis and on the count basis alike, and that combination separates succession from fragmentation in a way currency data cannot \citep{ChenDuffie2021Fragmentation}. Whether the role also changed hands turns on the basis of counting: leadership alternates on the value basis from 2022 onward, while the native asset leads in every year of the sample when use is counted by transaction. And architecture proved not to be decisive, since withdrawing the technical mandate to route through the native asset left native pairing where it was.

Four limits bound what has been established. Nothing is identified causally, and the validation apparatus that stands in for an identifying design is the weaker instrument. The matched sample is a small and non-random slice of realised routing, inverted on the dimension that drives the outcome, which is why the headline is a reweighted figure and why no result conditional on trade size generalises. The turnover hazard is estimated on a sixty-day window in which few pair-days carry an undominated incumbent, which leaves one arm of the comparison resting on a thin denominator that is reported alongside the ratio. And that window prices one venue family at the marginal price over a one-day step, while most multi-leg routing late in the sample crosses more than one venue, which measures the speed at which the role moves and leaves the pair's eventual destination open.

Two directions follow. The first is that the arbitrageur is a measuring instrument this paper excludes and should use. Round-trip cyclic routes are removed everywhere here as wash trading, and they are the one population that provably carries no habit, since a searcher re-optimises at every block and stops trading if it routes badly \citep{DaianEtAl2020FlashBoys,HeimbachPahariSchertenleib2024NonAtomic}. Which asset such a searcher opens and closes its cycles in is a revealed ranking of transaction cost with the stickiness stripped out, and comparing it against the acyclic population would give a habit-free benchmark for the vehicle role. The second is that this system's vehicle role changed hands once already inside six years, where the dollar's has not in eighty. \citet{FlandreauJobst2006Empirics} reach persistence without lock-in on a nineteenth-century network by counting the markets that quote each currency, and the quantity governing the speed of a turnover is measurable here in a way that counting cannot reach.
