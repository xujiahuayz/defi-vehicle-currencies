\section{Realised routes and execution costs}
\label{sec:executability}

Realised routes identify the assets that carried each trade. Explaining route choice through execution costs requires the alternatives available immediately before execution, quoted at the same trade size and over the venues the trader or router could reach. The route-composition results in Section~\ref{sec:dominance} follow directly from observed paths. The cost mechanism adds this counterfactual opportunity set.

Our price construction holds the endpoints, input amount, and pre-transaction pool state fixed and compares the output of direct and two-leg paths. The resulting comparison measures alternatives in public on-chain states. A router may also condition on private order flow, financing costs, the cost of a reverted transaction, and a search set narrower than the public market.

Pool balances and liquidity are measured immediately before the transaction by replaying swaps and liquidity events in their on-chain order; closing hourly or daily states can contain information that arrived after the route was chosen. Hypothetical trades are also restricted to size--depth combinations represented among realised swaps. Appendices~\ref{app:state} and~\ref{app:support} describe the reconstruction and its validation.

The counterfactual-price perimeter contains the pool families for which historical state and pricing rules can be reconstructed. Its largest uncovered group consists of Curve pools whose invariants remain unavailable to the quoter. These pools account for 34.2\% of assessed Curve volume and are concentrated in native-asset depth: 65.2\% of native-leg volume, compared with 21.1\% of stable-leg volume. Hook-bearing or dynamic-fee Uniswap V4 pools, SushiSwap V3 pools without historical state, and several Balancer pool families also lie outside the priced perimeter. An uncovered market may improve either a direct or an intermediated route, making the direction of selection ambiguous. Appendix~\ref{app:coverage} reports coverage by venue and asset type.
