We reconstruct decentralised-exchange routes and identify the intermediary currency. On the same January--June dates in 2024 and 2026, stablecoin share among exact two-leg routes with a sole native-or-stable intermediary rises from 16.9\% to 42.3\% by count and from 32.7\% to 76.5\% by supported value. Stablecoins lose ground in 2023 and 2024 before regaining the value lead in 2025. USDT becomes more common as an intermediary relative to endpoint use. Most of the rotation reflects trading shifts across source--destination asset pairs and changes in their use of indirect execution.
