We reconstruct \RoutePanelRawSwaps{} swaps and observe the vehicle currency inside each connected route. Between matched first halves of 2024 and 2026, pooled stablecoin share rises from \PairPooledBase{} to \PairPooledEnd{} by count and by \PairValueTotal{} by value. Net switching within continuing endpoint pairs is \PairPooledWithin{}; trading reallocation contributes \PairPooledReweight{} and period-specific pairs contribute \PairPooledExclusive{}. The entry vehicle predicts later allocation, but exact pretrade prices sort this persistence: retention is \PriceIncumbentLeaderRetention{} when the incumbent remains the price leader and \PriceChallengerIncumbentRetention{} when a challenger leads. Stablecoin use rises with capital on the weaker route leg. Dominance shifts as networks form around price-leading bridges, with little net replacement inside established pairs.
