I measure how one asset becomes the medium others trade through, using decentralised exchange, where every alternative path is priced and route selection is performed by optimisers carrying no memory of yesterday. I find that realised routing passes through an intermediary beaten on cost by a direct pool at the same size on a substantial share of trades, and that the role changes hands only slightly more often when its occupant is beaten than when it is cheapest. Across the sample the role spreads over more assets, while whether it also changes hands turns on counting use by value or by transaction.
