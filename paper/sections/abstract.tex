We reconstruct decentralised-exchange routes and identify the intermediary currency. Among routes with a native or stable intermediary, stablecoin share rises from 16.9\% to 42.3\% by count and from 32.7\% to 76.5\% by supported routed value. Stablecoins lose ground in 2023 and 2024 before regaining the value lead in 2025. USDT drives the increase and becomes more common as an intermediary relative to its endpoint use. Most of the rotation occurs as trading shifts across source--destination asset pairs and as those pairs change their use of indirect execution, making the allocation of trading across pairs central to changing vehicle dominance.
