We reconstruct \RoutePanelRawSwaps{} swaps and observe routes' vehicle currencies. From 2024 H1 to 2026 H1, stablecoin share rises from \PairPooledBase{} to \PairPooledEnd{}. Among pairs that trade again, entry vehicle identity predicts use through day 120. Holding pair, date, input, and pretrade state fixed, the stablecoin price-leadership coefficient is \ContestPriceLeaderEffect{}. Pair entry and activity reallocation drive aggregate rotation; prices and two-leg capital shape persistence.
