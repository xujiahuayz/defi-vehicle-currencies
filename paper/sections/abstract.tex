We reconstruct \RoutePanelRawSwaps{} swaps and observe the vehicle currency inside each connected route. From 2024 H1 to 2026 H1, stablecoin share among native and stable intermediaries rises from \PairPooledBase{} to \PairPooledEnd{}, an increase of 25.5 percentage points (pp). Net switching within continuing pairs is \PairPooledWithin{}, while pairs first observed after 2024 H1 contribute \PairLifecycleCountEntry{}. Among pairs that trade again, the entry vehicle predicts use through day 120. At the first sampled exact contest between stablecoin and WETH paths, the entry family still carries \FirstContestRouteRetention{} of routes and current exact output predicts which family survives. When the exact-output ranking changes hands, incumbent route share falls from \RankCrossingPriorShare{} to \RankCrossingCurrentShare{}; prior weak-leg capital predicts whether the new lead persists. Aggregate rotation therefore comes mainly from pair formation and activity reallocation. Inside established pairs, price competitiveness associated with two-leg depth maintains vehicle dominance, while exact-price rank reversals reallocate flow.
