Using transaction-level routes on decentralised exchanges, we observe the asset carrying each indirect trade. Stablecoins' route share rises from \StableCountBase{} to \StableCountEnd{} between the first halves of 2024 and 2026, while matched source--destination pairs show little stable-for-native substitution. The rotation is made at the market edge: entrants adopt stable vehicles, stable-born pairs stay stable, and USDC/USDT dominate stable entry. Local depth enters the mechanism: the deepest prior two-leg bridge carries \BridgeLiquidityTopShare{} of five-candidate route mass, and depth survives candidate-reach controls. Provider capital moves with the role, but not one-for-one: stable-route demand rises during the USDC/SVB window as stable capital falls.
