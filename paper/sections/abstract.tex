Using transaction-level routes on decentralised exchanges, we observe the asset carrying each indirect trade. Stablecoins' route share rises from \StableCountBase{} to \StableCountEnd{} between the first halves of 2024 and 2026. Matched pairs show little stable-for-native substitution; reallocation across markets carries the shift. The mechanism is market formation: entrants adopt stable vehicles, stable-born pairs stay stable, USDC/USDT dominate stable entry, and local two-leg bridge depth predicts route choice after candidate-reach controls. Stable route use also forecasts later bridge deepening. Provider capital moves with the role, but not one for one: stable-route demand rises during USDC/SVB as stable capital falls.
