We reconstruct \RoutePanelRawSwaps{} swaps and observe the vehicle currency inside each connected route. Between matched first halves of 2024 and 2026, pooled stablecoin share rises from \PairPooledBase{} to \PairPooledEnd{} by count and by \PairValueTotal{} by value. Net switching within continuing endpoint pairs is \PairPooledWithin{}; trading reallocation contributes \PairPooledReweight{} and period-specific pairs contribute \PairPooledExclusive{}. The vehicle first used by a new pair predicts its later allocation. Stablecoin use rises as the weaker route leg approaches WETH depth, and capital builds before first use. Exact pretrade repricing finds broader competition mainly across venues using the same vehicle. Dominance shifts as trading relationships form around different bridges.
