We reconstruct transaction-level routes on decentralised exchanges to observe which assets carry trades between others. Among native and stablecoin intermediaries, stablecoins' route share rises from 16.9\% to 42.3\% between the first halves of 2024 and 2026; their dollar-weighted share rises from 32.7\% to 76.5\%. Native assets regain share in 2023 and 2024 before stablecoins recover the value lead. USDT's intermediary use also grows relative to its observed route-endpoint use. Most of the rotation reflects activity shifting toward source--destination pairs routed through stablecoins; estimated stable-for-native substitution within matched pairs is close to zero.
