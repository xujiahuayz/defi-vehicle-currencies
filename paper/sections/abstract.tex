We reconstruct decentralised-exchange routes and identify the currency used between the assets bought and sold. Among routes with a native or stable intermediary, stablecoin share rises from 16.9\% to 42.3\% by count and from 32.7\% to 76.5\% by value. Stablecoins lose ground in 2023 and 2024 before regaining the value lead in 2025. USDT drives the increase and becomes more common as an intermediary relative to its endpoint use. Most of the rotation occurs as trading shifts across endpoint markets and as those markets change their use of indirect execution, making market allocation central to changing vehicle dominance.
