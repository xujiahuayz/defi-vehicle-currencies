We use transaction-level routes on decentralised exchanges to measure vehicle-currency dominance. From 2024 to the first half of 2026, stablecoins' share of routes intermediated by native assets or stablecoins rises from 16.9\% to 42.3\% by count and from 32.7\% to 76.5\% by routed value. The rotation is not monotonic. Most of the increase reflects changes in which endpoint markets trade, how activity shifts across continuing markets, and whether trades use an intermediary; stable-for-native substitution within continuing markets contributes little. USDT drives the increase and becomes more prevalent as an intermediary relative to its use as the asset bought or sold.
