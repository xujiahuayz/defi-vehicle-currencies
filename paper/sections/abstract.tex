We study how vehicle-currency dominance changes when the intermediary is observed on each transaction. Decentralised exchanges record the full trading route, so we can distinguish an asset's intermediary use from demand to buy or sell it. From 2020 to 2026, vehicle use rotates sharply but unevenly from the native asset toward fiat-backed stablecoins, with USDT accounting for most of the increase. The rotation occurs mainly because routed activity moves toward endpoint markets where stable intermediation is present and because those markets use intermediaries more often. Stable-for-native substitution within continuing markets contributes little. Vehicle dominance therefore develops through reallocation across markets as well as currency choice within them.
