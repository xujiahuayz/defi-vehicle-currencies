We reconstruct transaction-level routes on decentralised exchanges to observe which assets carry trades between others. Among native and stablecoin intermediaries, stablecoins' route share rises from 16.9\% to 42.3\% between the first halves of 2024 and 2026, and their dollar-weighted share from 32.7\% to 76.5\%. What changes is the trading network, not the intermediary chosen: within matched ultimate (source--destination) pairs, stable-for-native substitution is close to zero, while activity reallocates toward ultimate pairs already routed through stablecoins and newly traded pairs adopt them. Native assets regain share in 2023--2024, and USDT's intermediary use grows relative to its route-endpoint use.
