Transaction-level routes in \ac{defi} reveal the asset that carries each routed trade. Stablecoin dominance rises from \StableCountBase{} to \StableCountEnd{} of routes between early 2024 and 2026. Net stable-for-native substitution within continuing ultimate pairs is close to zero: reweighting to stablecoin-intensive pairs and pairs active in only one comparison year account for the aggregate rise. After a persistent stablecoin bridge forms, stable route share rises with relative depth and is \BridgeDepthEqualFirstShare{} when stable depth is at least WETH depth. Trading-network composition and local liquidity formation therefore shape vehicle-currency dominance.
