Transaction-level decentralised-exchange routes reveal which asset carries each indirect trade. Stablecoins' route share rises from \StableCountBase{} to \StableCountEnd{} between the first halves of 2024 and 2026, while activity shifts across ultimate pairs carry most of the aggregate increase.
