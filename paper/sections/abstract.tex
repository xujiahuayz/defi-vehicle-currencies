We reconstruct routes from 475 million Ethereum decentralised-exchange swaps. Stablecoins' share among native-or-stable two-leg routes rises 25.5 percentage points (pp) from 2024 H1 to 2026 H1, yet net substitution within continuing pairs is \(-0.1\) pp; entry and reallocation account for it. Stable-facing v3 additions rotate across transaction origins; prior same-vehicle participation predicts new-pool participation. Before stablecoin use, two-leg capital predicts 1.84 pp higher weekly first-use probability; conditional on support, a tenfold stablecoin/WETH capital ratio adds 1.84 pp. Relative capital predicts allocation and one-month price-lead persistence. ETH declines revalue fixed-pool capital; monthly route-capital, exact-output, and use coefficients are near zero.
