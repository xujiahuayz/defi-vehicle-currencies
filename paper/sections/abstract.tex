Decentralised exchanges record the intermediary asset on each realised trading route, allowing vehicle use to be measured separately from ordinary endpoint demand. Using transaction-level routes from 2020 to 2026, I document a sharp, non-monotonic rotation in vehicle intermediation from the native asset toward fiat-backed stablecoins. The shift is larger in routed value than in route counts and stronger in cross-venue trades. USDT drives the change within the stablecoin category, which remains visible after conditioning on observed venue span and route complexity. The routes identify a change in the intermediary role; whether routing technology, liquidity placement, or monetary coordination caused it remains open.
