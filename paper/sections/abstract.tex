Decentralised exchanges record the intermediary asset on each realised trading route, allowing vehicle use to be separated from ordinary endpoint demand. Using transaction-level routes from 2020 to 2026, I document a sharp but non-monotonic rotation from the native asset toward fiat-backed stablecoins. USDT accounts for most of the change. Stablecoin dominance grows mainly as new endpoint markets enter, trading shifts across continuing markets, and those markets use intermediaries more often. Direct stable-for-native substitution within a continuing market contributes little. Vehicle dominance therefore changes through the allocation of markets and routed activity, not only through repeated currency choice within a fixed market.
