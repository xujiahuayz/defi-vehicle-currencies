Decentralised exchanges record the intermediary asset on each realised trading route, allowing vehicle use to be separated from ordinary endpoint demand. Using transaction-level routes from 2020 to 2026, I document a sharp but non-monotonic rotation from the native asset toward fiat-backed stablecoins. The aggregate increase comes almost entirely from changes in which endpoint pairs carry vehicle routes and shifts in activity among continuing pairs; stable shares within continuing pairs barely change. USDT accounts for most of the increase within the stablecoin category. Whether route availability, liquidity placement, or monetary coordination causes this reallocation remains open.
