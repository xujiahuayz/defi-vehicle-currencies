I study how a vehicle currency emerges and changes when the trading network and realised routes are observable. Decentralised exchange records which assets carry indirect trades and permits vehicle use to be separated from ordinary endpoint demand. Vehicle dominance rotates sharply from the platform's native asset toward fiat-backed stablecoins. The transition is late, non-monotonic, and strongest in cross-venue routing. It survives stratification by route integration and complexity, but current architecture events do not provide clean causal contrasts. The evidence separates an asset's intermediary role from its use as a payment endpoint and shows how routing technology can activate, rather than merely explain away, monetary coordination.
