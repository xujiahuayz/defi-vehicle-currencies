I measure how a currency comes to be the one others trade through, using decentralised exchange, where every alternative path is observable and routing is performed by optimisers that hold no habit. I find that assets keep the vehicle role at moments when routing through them is not the cheapest way to trade, and I ask how long the role outlives the advantage, comparing the time an incumbent holds on against the time a challenger takes to break in. Across the sample the role spreads across steadily more assets, while whether it also changes hands depends on whether use is counted by value or by transaction.
