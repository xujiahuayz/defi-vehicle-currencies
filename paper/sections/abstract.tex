We reconstruct routes from 475 million Ethereum decentralised-exchange swaps. Stablecoin use rises 25.5 percentage points (pp) from 2024 H1 to 2026 H1, yet net substitution within continuing pairs is -0.1 pp; entry and reallocation explain the rise. Stable-facing Uniswap v3 additions rotate across providers; prior vehicle experience predicts new-pool participation. Before first stablecoin use, two-leg support predicts 1.84 pp higher weekly adoption; among supported pairs, a tenfold stablecoin/WETH capital ratio predicts another 1.84 pp. Relative capital predicts allocation and persistent price leadership. ETH declines revalue standing capital but barely predict monthly route capital, exact output, or vehicle use.
