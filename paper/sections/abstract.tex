We use \RoutePanelRawSwaps{} swaps to observe vehicle currencies in transaction-level routes on decentralised exchanges. Between matched January--June periods in 2024 and 2026, stablecoins' route-count share among native and stable vehicles rises from \StableCountBase{} to \StableCountEnd{}. Stable-for-native moves offset within continuing endpoint pairs. The increase comes from trading shifting toward continuing pairs with greater stablecoin use and from pairs present in only one period. The vehicle used at pair entry predicts later allocation. Stablecoin use rises sharply as both legs of its route deepen relative to WETH. Exact price competition changes venues much more often than vehicles.
