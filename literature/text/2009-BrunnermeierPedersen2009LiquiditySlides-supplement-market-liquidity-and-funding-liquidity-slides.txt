Liquidity Dry-ups/Fragility
Deﬁnition 1
Liquidity is fragile if the price correspondence p∗
t (η1, vt) is
discontinuous in ηt or vt.
Proposition 1
(i) With informed ﬁnanciers, the market is fragile at time 1 if
x0 is large enough.
(ii) With uninformed ﬁnanciers, the market is fragile at time 1
if x0 large enough or if margins are increasing enough with
illiquidity Λ 1. The latter happens if θ is large enough (i.e.
ARCH eﬀects are strong) and the ﬁnancier’s prior on a
fundamental shock (1 − a) is large enough (i.e. a < ¯a).
