# Literature discovery and decision-history digests

This directory keeps bounded synthesis records that remain useful for source discovery or reconstructing earlier research decisions but do not satisfy the current literature gate. That discovery and reconstruction role is a real agent consumer; these files are not disposable merely because they are historical.

- `historical-nbc-corpus-manifest.md` records the Nanyang pipeline's original comparator and co-author scan.
- `historical-nbc-source-fidelity.md` preserves its early paired-read reconciliations.
- `historical-nbc-domain-literature.md` preserves the domain sweep that redirected that pipeline.

These files are not citation or claim authority. Current admission lives in `literature/source-admission.json`; citation metadata in `literature/vehicle-currencies.bib`; source-family dispositions in `literature/source-notes/`; individual current review cards and their index in `literature/reviews/current/`; and reconciled claim, structure, depth, breadth and optics decisions in `literature/audit.md`.

A future agent reading a historical digest is consuming it for discovery, not as authoritative claim support. It must route any reusable scientific point through the current source-set audit before changing a claim, specification, paper, or deck. When a current source-family record fully incorporates a search trail or decision, remove the duplicated passage here; do not preserve parallel summaries indefinitely.
