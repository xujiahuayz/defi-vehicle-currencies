# Historical literature digests

This directory keeps bounded synthesis records that remain useful for source discovery or reconstructing earlier research decisions but do not satisfy the current literature gate.

- `legacy-nbc-corpus-manifest.md` records the Nanyang pipeline's original comparator and co-author scan.
- `legacy-nbc-source-fidelity.md` preserves its early paired-read reconciliations.
- `legacy-nbc-domain-literature.md` preserves the domain sweep that redirected that pipeline.

These files are not citation or claim authority. Current admission lives in `literature/source-admission.json`; citation metadata in `literature/vehicle-currencies.bib`; source-family dispositions in `literature/source-notes/`; individual current review cards and their index in `docs/reviews/`; and reconciled claim, structure, depth, breadth and optics decisions in `docs/literature-audit.md`.

A future agent reading a historical digest is performing discovery, not consuming an authoritative input. It must route any reusable point through the current source-set audit before changing a claim, specification, paper, or deck.
