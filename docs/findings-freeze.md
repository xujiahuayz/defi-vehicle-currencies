## Definition guard

- **Vehicle status is binary:** an asset is or is not used as a non-endpoint intermediary on a route.
- **Vehicle dominance is continuous:** it is the degree of vehicle use, measured by intermediary share and, after netting out endpoint demand, the vehicle excess-use ratio.
- **Cost domination is separate:** a realised or counterfactual route is cost-dominated when an available alternative returns more at the same reconstructed state. This object is never called `dominance` without the cost qualifier.
- **Abstract contract:** the paper asks what makes a vehicle currency dominant. It does not ask what makes an asset a vehicle currency.
