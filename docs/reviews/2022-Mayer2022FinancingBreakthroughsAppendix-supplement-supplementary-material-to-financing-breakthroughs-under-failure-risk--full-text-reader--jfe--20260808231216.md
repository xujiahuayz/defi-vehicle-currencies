- Scientific: The reported model studies dynamic financing when an agent exerts effort and may privately observe project failure. Its claimed contribution over static bad-news-disclosure models is that continuation utility links disclosure incentives across time, producing an initial unconditional-financing/no-disclosure stage followed by a full-disclosure stage with decreasing rewards for reported failure (response PDF pp.1–2, 11–14). Public observability with probability π>0 is load-bearing: π=0 yields full disclosure from inception, whereas π>0 makes initial full disclosure costly because some agents would receive unnecessary rents (p.18). The response claims global contract optimality after ruling out fake failure, randomized disclosure, and non-full effort under Λpμ>κ+φ and φ<κ (pp.12–15), but the proofs reside in the unread manuscript. With discounting, the reported state equation is \(\dot w=rw-\phi\); the author claims the optimum keeps \(w<\phi/r\), hence decreasing rewards and the two-stage structure (pp.11–12). Other reported robustness exercises allow endogenous completion timing, hidden success, and continuous effort (pp.2–3).
