# Finding candidate: direct connectivity and excess path position

Status: provisional E0 diagnostic. The build is bound to the exact current unified tree and must be rerun after the refreshed D2 route ledger is installed. It covers 95 of 98 registered 24-day-stride dates from 2020-04-23 through 2026-06-27; the three earlier dates have no graph with at least four nodes after the clean-route contract is applied.

## Measurement contract

Each date supplies an undirected token-pair graph from clean, non-cyclic reconstructed route components. Native ETH and WETH are one currency. Topological degree counts directly linked markets. Count strength sums clean transaction legs on incident pairs. Value strength sums incident USD value only when the source, every intermediary and sink reconcile within 20 percent; the value graph additionally requires at least $1,000 of supported pair-day value. Eigenvector centrality is computed on the largest connected component, with zero assigned outside it. Betweenness uses topological, inverse-count and inverse-value path lengths. Every direct, eigenvector and betweenness statistic is normalized to a within-day share before constructing excess path position as betweenness share minus the matching direct or eigenvector share. Raw centrality statistics on unrelated scales are never subtracted.

This separates three economic objects: direct market breadth or activity, the importance of a token's direct neighbors, and the token's position on paths between other assets. It also separates these graph objects from realized intermediary use. The historical columns `betweenness_centrality` and `volume_weighted_betweenness` measure intent-route intermediary incidence, not graph-theoretic betweenness; the variable registry now names them accordingly.

## Provisional result

The graph preserves a large structural role for the native currency while realized intermediation rotates toward stables. From 2024 to 2026, the native asset's mean betweenness share falls from 86.8% to 76.7% topologically, 84.2% to 73.2% by count and 77.9% to 61.1% by supported value. Stable shares rise from 9.3% to 18.2%, 11.6% to 21.4% and 16.1% to 30.5%, respectively. Over the same dates, stable direct count strength rises from 8.6% to 24.8% and direct value strength from 32.1% to 49.6%, while native direct count and value strength fall from 46.5% to 36.7% and 37.3% to 22.0%.

The direct-versus-path decomposition sharpens the distinction. Native betweenness remains far above its direct position in 2026: +41.4 percentage points over topological degree share, +36.5 points over count strength share and +39.1 points over value strength share. Stable excess path position is +7.5 points topologically, -3.4 points by count and -19.1 points by value. Stable assets therefore carry more direct economic activity than their shortest-path position would predict, while the native asset remains unusually important to network reach even after its direct activity share is netted out.

The measures agree on level but not on excess role. Across 2,542 currency token-date matches, graph count betweenness and realized intermediary count share have Spearman correlation 0.683; graph value betweenness and strict-support intermediary value share correlate 0.689. Once each is normalized against its own benchmark, the agreement disappears: excess count path position versus endpoint-normalized count use has correlation 0.035, and excess value path position versus endpoint-normalized strict-value use has correlation -0.090. These are distinct constructs, not interchangeable robustness measures.

## Interpretation and next attacks

The current evidence supports a two-layer transition: stablecoins increasingly carry observed vehicle flows and direct market activity, while WETH remains the architectural connector of the token graph. That gap is a candidate mechanism result because it distinguishes flow rotation from installed network structure. Before promotion, rerun on the certified D2 generation; test edge-support thresholds by count and strict value; replace sampled betweenness with exact or convergence-bounded estimates on a smaller validation calendar; quantify sampling noise across seeds; split single- and cross-venue graphs; and test whether the structural-use gap predicts route regret, liquidity allocation or subsequent vehicle use. Betweenness, degree, strength and eigenvector measures remain diagnostics until those attacks survive.
