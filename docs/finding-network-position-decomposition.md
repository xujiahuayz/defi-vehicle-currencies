# Finding candidate: direct prominence and path-position contrasts

Status: provisional E0 diagnostic. The build is bound to the exact current unified tree and must be rerun after the refreshed D2 route ledger is installed. It covers 95 of 98 registered 24-day-stride dates from 2020-04-23 through 2026-06-27; the three earlier dates have no graph with at least four nodes after the clean-route contract is applied.

## Measurement contract

Each date supplies an undirected token-pair graph from clean, non-cyclic reconstructed route components. Native ETH and WETH are one currency. Topological degree counts directly linked markets. Count-weighted degree, also called count strength, sums clean transaction legs on incident pairs. Value-weighted degree, also called value strength, sums incident USD value only when the source, every intermediary and sink reconcile within 20 percent; the value graph additionally requires at least $1,000 of supported pair-day value. Eigenvector centrality is computed on the largest connected component, with zero assigned outside it. Betweenness uses topological, inverse-count and inverse-value path lengths. Every direct, eigenvector and betweenness statistic is normalized to a within-day share before calculating path-minus-direct and path-minus-neighbor contrasts. Raw centrality statistics on unrelated scales are never subtracted.

This separates three economic objects: direct market breadth or activity, the importance of a token's direct neighbors, and the token's position on paths between other assets. Degree and eigenvector centrality include endpoint prominence and are not vehicle-role measures by themselves. Standard betweenness excludes the focal token as an origin or destination, but count- and value-weighted path lengths still inherit realized activity on incident edges. Normalized path-minus-direct and path-minus-neighbor differences are therefore descriptive contrasts, not endpoint-neutral excess dominance. A publishable network vehicle measure must additionally benchmark each statistic against endpoint demand or a prespecified null model conditional on network size, venue reach and relevant marginals. The historical columns `betweenness_centrality` and `volume_weighted_betweenness` measure intent-route intermediary incidence, not graph-theoretic betweenness; the variable registry now names them accordingly.

## Provisional result

The realized trading graph retains a large path-position share for the native currency while realized intermediation rotates toward stables. From 2024 to 2026, the native asset's mean betweenness share falls from 86.8% to 76.7% topologically, 84.2% to 73.2% by count and 77.9% to 61.1% by supported value. Stable shares rise from 9.3% to 18.2%, 11.6% to 21.4% and 16.1% to 30.5%, respectively. Over the same dates, stable direct count strength rises from 8.6% to 24.8% and direct value strength from 32.1% to 49.6%, while native direct count and value strength fall from 46.5% to 36.7% and 37.3% to 22.0%.

The direct-versus-path decomposition sharpens the descriptive distinction. Native betweenness remains far above its direct position in 2026: +41.4 percentage points over topological degree share, +36.5 points over count strength share and +39.1 points over value strength share. That contrast survives excluding token pairs with fewer than five or 25 clean legs: the native count contrast remains +32.1 and +32.7 points. The stable count contrast is threshold-sensitive around zero, moving from -3.3 points with every clean pair to +1.1 and +0.7 points under the five- and 25-leg screens, so its sign carries no interpretation. The value contrast is stable across minimum supported pair-day values of $100, $1,000 and $10,000: native path-minus-direct value position is +42.4, +39.1 and +30.5 points in 2026, while the stable contrast is -20.3, -19.1 and -13.0 points. Direct-value rotation is nearly invariant across those thresholds, with native declining 15.2 to 15.3 points and stable rising 17.5 to 17.6 points from 2024 to 2026. These patterns motivate an endpoint-demand-adjusted network test; they do not yet establish installed structural dependence because the graph itself is formed from realized traded edges.

The measures agree on level but not on excess role. Across 2,542 currency token-date matches, graph count betweenness and realized intermediary count share have Spearman correlation 0.683; graph value betweenness and strict-support intermediary value share correlate 0.689. Once each is normalized against its own benchmark, the agreement disappears: excess count path position versus endpoint-normalized count use has correlation 0.035, and excess value path position versus endpoint-normalized strict-value use has correlation -0.090. These are distinct constructs, not interchangeable robustness measures.

## Interpretation and next attacks

The current evidence suggests a two-layer transition: stablecoins increasingly carry observed vehicle flows and direct market activity, while WETH retains more path position in the realized trading graph. Before promotion, construct endpoint-demand-adjusted direct, neighbor and path measures under a prespecified null; rebuild the feasible graph from pair availability or exact quote/liquidity state where support permits; rerun on the certified D2 generation; replace sampled betweenness with exact or convergence-bounded estimates on a smaller validation calendar; quantify sampling noise across seeds; split single- and cross-venue graphs; and test whether the adjusted path-use gap predicts route regret, liquidity allocation or subsequent vehicle use. Betweenness, degree, weighted degree/strength and eigenvector measures remain diagnostics until those attacks survive.
