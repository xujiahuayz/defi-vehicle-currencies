# Retired: the single-venue empirical round (2026-08-04), and what survives it

**Status: RETIRED. All scripts and numeric outputs from this round are deleted, not archived.** They measured liquidity on Uniswap V3 alone and would mislead anyone who found them. Recoverable from git history at `a8186e6` if ever needed, but they should not be reused. This file exists so the five things worth keeping are not lost with them.

## Why the whole round was retired

`src/ddvc/analysis/lp_concentration.py` reads only `data/raw/thegraph/uniswap_v3/`, so candidate-linked liquidity $L_{k,t}$ was a Uniswap-V3-only quantity. The architecture event study compared Uniswap V2 against Uniswap V3. Both ignored SushiSwap v2/v3, Curve, Balancer, Uniswap v1/v4, and Fluid, all of which sit on disk. Routers split single trades across these venues, so a venue-siloed measure of "how much liquidity is linked to a candidate asset" does not measure the economic object the paper is about. Redo everything on the consolidated cross-venue layer.

## The five things worth keeping

**1. The wrong-signed cost coefficient is probably the paper's most interesting result, not a defect.** The retired panel found route volume through a candidate rising when the *direct* route was cheaper: sign opposite to a pure cost story, robust across three of four robustness variants, significant at two of four horizons. Read as a cost mechanism it fails. Read as incumbency it is the finding: routing does not follow contemporaneous relative cost. That is the Krugman/Eichengreen inertia question, which FX cannot settle for lack of an observable counterfactual and on-chain data can, because the cost of the road not taken is computable. The old July deck separately carried a lagged-share coefficient of 0.363 buried as a control, which is the same hysteresis object. Promote this to a designed test, do not re-derive it as a by-product.

**2. Any event study on the May 2021 Uniswap V3 launch inherits a confound.** A market-wide volatility episode (the Jan-May 2021 cycle) peaks in the launch month. Raw monthly mean absolute day-to-day change in route share runs ~16 in Oct-Dec 2020, steps to ~21 in Jan 2021, peaks ~22 in May 2021, and falls back to ~15 by Jul 2021. Consequence: placebo dates in that window produce "effects" as large as the true launch, and a joint Wald test on pre-period month dummies rejects flat pretrends even when the linear pretrend slope looks flat. Any future V3-launch design needs either a control group that shares the macro episode (cross-venue difference-in-differences does exactly this) or an outcome not dominated by generic volatility.

**3. Cross-venue is the design fix, and it also removes the mechanical-result problem.** Within-venue architecture effects are close to definitional: V3 pools did not exist before launch, so their share rising is arithmetic. Spillover to venues that did *not* change is not mechanical, and it shares the macro episode with the treated venue, which addresses (2). Templates already in the read corpus: Bessembinder, Hao and Zheng (off-venue spillovers from a liquidity-provision contract) and Klein and Song (cross-venue commonality following Chi-X entry).

**4. A verification technique that worked and should be reused.** When the placebo test failed, the question was whether the estimator was buggy or the design was confounded. It was settled by confirming the headline and placebo runs execute the identical estimator code path: a selective bug is then impossible, so the failure has to be a property of the data. Cheap, decisive, and reusable whenever a robustness check fails and the cause is ambiguous.

**5. Data facts worth not rediscovering.** `data/processed/observations_token_day.parquet` covers 2020-02-11 to 2026-06-30, 11,385 rows, keyed by (date, token), and already carries route-share, depth, and cost fields plus lag/lead features. Raw file counts on disk: sushiswap_v2 8,504, uniswap_v1 8,394, uniswap_v2 4,568, uniswap_v4 1,106, uniswap_v3 155, curve 90, balancer 90, sushiswap_v3 90. `sigma_pre` (pre-event pair volatility) is named in the variable registry but was never constructed; the retired round proxied it, which is a gap to close properly. The September 2020 UNI liquidity-mining launch is a genuine candidate exogenous supply shock (four V2 pools, all pairing the native asset against the stable numéraires and the wrapped store of value), but it predates any V3-only measure, so it is usable only once liquidity is measured on V2 as well.
