# Generated Exhibits

This directory contains tracked machine-readable or rendered evidence used for inspection and deliverable generation. Each current exhibit must have one identifiable producer, reopen its released inputs, and carry or link to provenance that binds its actual payload. The paper and deck may consume an exhibit only after the corresponding finding passes the research graph.

Many files here predate the current D3 ownership model. Their presence does not make them current. The active owner and cleanup rules are recorded in [`../../docs/repository-data-map.md`](../../docs/repository-data-map.md); stale, unregistered, or superseded exhibits should be removed only after a consumer and reachability audit.
