$\mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}$ and $\mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ are route-leg USD volumes for which $k$ is the leg's input or output token, respectively, on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ & USD & Input- and output-endpoint restrictions of $\mathrm{Vol}_t$ for token $k$: $0\le\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}\le\mathrm{Vol}_t$ and $0\le\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}\le\mathrm{Vol}_t$. \\

$\mathrm{Capital}_{p,t},\ C_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{Capital}_{p,t}$ is pool $p$'s validated day-$t$ accounting capital under its protocol contract. $C_{k,t}=\sum_{p\in\mathcal L_{k,t}}\mathrm{Capital}_{p,t}/m_p$; a one-candidate pool contributes all capital to that candidate and a two-candidate pool contributes one half to each. \\
$\mathrm{PoolVol}_{p,t},\ \mathrm{SpokeIVol}_{p,k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{PoolVol}_{p,t}$ is total realized swap volume in pool $p$ on day $t$. $\mathrm{SpokeIVol}_{p,k,t}$ restricts it to pool legs belonging to reconstructed indirect routes that use $k$ as the intermediate, so $0\le\mathrm{SpokeIVol}_{p,k,t}\le\mathrm{PoolVol}_{p,t}$. \\

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Route-cost quote objects}} \\
$\mathcal{P}_{k,t,q}$ & Set of token pairs & Quote-universe pairs for candidate $k$: distinct ordered input--output pairs among day $t$'s 200 largest clean reconstructed pairs by realized USD volume, where clean means route class \texttt{single} or \texttt{coherent}, $k\notin\{i,o\}$, and each of $i$, $o$, and $k$ has a valid day-price estimate. A valid estimate requires at least three finite token-side USD-per-token observations in $(0,\$1{,}000{,}000)$, at least 75 percent of observations within a fivefold band around their ordinary median, and equals the realized-USD-volume-weighted median inside that consensus band. Each pair is submitted to the direct and via-$k$ quote engines at input $q$; membership does not require either quote to execute. \\
$\mathcal{D}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{I}_{k,t,q}$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal D_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ and $\mathcal I_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ restrict the broad pair set to pairs with an executable direct route or an executable indirect route via $k$. \\
$\mathcal{C}_{k,t,q}$ & Set of token pairs & Common-support pairs for candidate $k$, day $t$, and notional $q$: $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}=\mathcal D_{k,t,q}\cap\mathcal I_{k,t,q}$, so both the direct and indirect routes are executable. \\
$\mathcal{T}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{W}_{k,t,q}$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal T_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal D_{k,t,q}$ is the thin-direct subset. $\mathcal W_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$ contains common-support pairs for which the indirect route beats the direct route, equivalently $\Delta C^D_{i,o,k,q,t}<0$. \\
$D_{i,o,q,t},\ I_{i,o,k,q,t},\ T_{i,o,q,t}$ & Indicator (0/1) & Indicators for direct-route availability ($D$), indirect-route-through-$k$ availability ($I$), and an executable direct route returning less than $0.9q$ ($T$). \\
$O^{D}_{i,o,q,t},\ O^{I}_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & USD & Quoted output values; superscripts $D$ and $I$ denote direct and indirect routes. \\
$O^{D,\mathrm{fee}}_{i,o,q,t},\ O^{I,\mathrm{fee}}_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & USD & Counterfactual direct and indirect output values when each pool remains at its pre-quote marginal price but charges its historical swap fee; these retain fee loss and suppress price impact. \\
$G^{D}_{i,o,q,t},\ G^{I}_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & USD & Historical gas expenditure for the quoted direct and indirect routes, respectively, using route-specific gas usage and the day-$t$ gas-token USD price. \\
$C^{D}_{i,o,q,t},\ C^{I}_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & Fraction & All-in execution-cost fractions for the direct and indirect alternatives, including quoted pool fees and price impact plus historical gas expenditure. \\
$\Delta C^D_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & Fraction & Pair-level direct cost advantage $(O^{D}_{i,o,q,t}-O^{I}_{i,o,k,q,t})/O^{D}_{i,o,q,t}$ on $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$; positive values favor the direct route. \\
$\Delta C^{D,\mathrm{all}}_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & Fraction & All-in direct cost advantage $C^{I}_{i,o,k,q,t}-C^{D}_{i,o,q,t}$ on $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$; positive values favor the direct route. \\
