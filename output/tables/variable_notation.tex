\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Indices}} \\
$i,\ j$ & Token & Input (sold) and output (bought) endpoint tokens; the ordered pair is $i\to j$. \\
$k$ & Token & Candidate vehicle token used as the route intermediate; $k\notin\{i,j\}$. \\
$\ell,\ p$ & Token / pool & $\ell$ indexes tokens in cross-token sums; $p$ indexes liquidity pools. \\
$t,\ w$ & UTC day / UTC week & $t$ indexes calendar days; $w$ indexes calendar weeks in settlement variables. \\

$r$ & Route unit & Receipt-audited coherent multihop route unit indexed by $r$. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Route and liquidity aggregates}} \\
$A_t,\ \mathrm{DirectRouteVolume}_t,\ \mathrm{IndirectRouteVolume}_t$ & USD & $\mathrm{DirectRouteVolume}_t$ and $\mathrm{IndirectRouteVolume}_t$ are day-$t$ realized volumes over direct and indirect route units; $A_t$ is their sum. \\
$B_{k,t},\ B_t$ & USD & $B_{k,t}$ is realized indirect-route volume through vehicle $k$; $B_t=\mathrm{IndirectRouteVolume}_t$ is total indirect-route volume on day $t$. \\
$N^B_{k,t},\ N^B_t$ & Route-unit count & $N^B_{k,t}$ counts indirect route units through $k$; $N^B_t$ counts all indirect route units on day $t$; superscript $B$ denotes bridged (indirect) routes. \\
$\mathcal A_t,\ \mathcal A^k_t,\ \mathcal M^k_t$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal A_t$ is the set of active endpoint pairs; $\mathcal A^k_t$ is the subset using $k$; $\mathcal M^k_t$ is the subset for which $k$ has the largest realized indirect-route volume. \\
$\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}$ and $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ are inbound and outbound route-leg volumes assigned to token $k$ on day $t$. \\
$N^{\mathrm{route}}_t,\ N^{\mathrm{mid}}_{k,t},\ N^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t},\ N^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}$ & Route count & $N^{\mathrm{route}}_t$ counts all day-$t$ intent routes; $N^{\mathrm{mid}}_{k,t}$, $N^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t}$, and $N^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}$ count routes assigning $k$ the intermediate, source, and sink roles. \\
$\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{route}}_t,\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{mid}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{route}}_t$ is total day-$t$ intent-route volume; $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{mid}}_{k,t}$, $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t}$, and $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}$ assign that volume to $k$ by route role. \\
$L_{k,t},\ \mathcal K$ & USD / set of tokens & $L_{k,t}$ is vehicle-linked liquidity for $k$ on day $t$; $\mathcal K$ is the candidate-vehicle set. \\
$\mathcal L_{k,t},\ \mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}$ & Set of pools / USD & $\mathcal L_{k,t}$ is the set of eligible pools linked to $k$ on day $t$; $\mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}$ is total value locked in pool $p$ on that day. \\
$R^{\mathrm{WETH}}_t$ & Daily log-return fraction & Day-$t$ log return of the WETH price used to construct downside stress. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Route-cost quote objects}} \\
$\mathcal{P}_{k,t,q}$ & Set of token pairs & Ordered endpoint pairs eligible for $k$ and quoted on day $t$ at notional $q$. \\
$\mathcal{D}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{V}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{C}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{T}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{W}_{k,t,q}$ & Sets of token pairs & Subsets of $\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ with a direct route ($\mathcal D$), a route via $k$ ($\mathcal V$), both routes ($\mathcal C=\mathcal D\cap\mathcal V$), a thin direct route ($\mathcal T$), or a vehicle-route cost advantage ($\mathcal W$). \\
$D_{i,j,q,t},\ V_{i,j,k,q,t},\ T_{i,j,q,t}$ & Indicator (0/1) & Indicators for direct-route availability ($D$), route-through-$k$ availability ($V$), and an executable direct route returning less than $0.9q$ ($T$). \\
$O^{D}_{i,j,q,t},\ O^{V}_{i,j,k,q,t}$ & USD & Quoted output values; superscripts $D$ and $V$ denote direct and vehicle routes. \\
$\Delta C_{i,j,k,q,t}$ & Basis points & $10{,}000\,(O^{V}_{i,j,k,q,t}-O^{D}_{i,j,q,t})/O^{D}_{i,j,q,t}$ on $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Settlement objects and operators}} \\
$\mathcal{R}_{k,w}$ & Set of route units & Receipt-audited matched route units using vehicle $k$ in UTC week $w$. \\
$\mathcal{R}^{\mathrm{transfer}}_{k,w}$ & Set of route units & Members of $\mathcal R_{k,w}$ whose receipt logs a transfer of vehicle $k$. \\
$|\mathcal{A}|,\ \mathbf{1}\{\cdot\}$ & Count / indicator (0/1) & Cardinality of set $\mathcal A$ and an indicator equal to one when its condition is true. \\
$\mathrm{Betweenness}^{\mathrm{vol}},\ \Delta_{7}$ & USD weighting / 7 days & Superscript $\mathrm{vol}$ denotes USD weighting; for any daily variable $X_t$, $\Delta_7 X_t=X_{t+7}-X_t$. \\

\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Vehicle-use measures}} \\
$\mathrm{VehicleShare}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{B_{k,t}}{B_t}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{bridge\_\allowbreak{}share} & $B_{k,t}$ is indirect-route USD volume through $k$; $B_t$ is total indirect-route USD volume on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{AllRouteVehicleShare}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{B_{k,t}}{A_t}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{all\_\allowbreak{}route\_\allowbreak{}bridge\_\allowbreak{}share} & $B_{k,t}$ is indirect-route USD volume through $k$; $A_t$ is total direct plus indirect route USD volume on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{VehicleCountShare}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{N^{B}_{k,t}}{N^{B}_{t}}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{bridge\_\allowbreak{}count\_\allowbreak{}share} & $N^{B}_{k,t}$ counts indirect route units through $k$; $N^{B}_{t}$ counts all indirect route units on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{PairCoverage}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal A^{k}_{t}|}{|\mathcal A_t|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{pair\_\allowbreak{}coverage} & $\mathcal A_t$ is the set of active endpoint pairs; $\mathcal A^k_t$ contains pairs using $k$ in at least one indirect route on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{MainVehiclePairShare}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal M^{k}_{t}|}{|\mathcal A_t|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{pair\_\allowbreak{}main\_\allowbreak{}vehicle\_\allowbreak{}share} & $\mathcal M^k_t\subseteq\mathcal A_t$ contains pairs for which $k$ has the largest realized indirect-route USD volume on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{VehicleVolume}_{k,t}$ & $B_{k,t}$ & USD & \texttt{bridge\_\allowbreak{}volume\_\allowbreak{}usd} & Sum of realized USD volume over indirect routes using vehicle $k$ on day $t$. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Network and route-denominator controls}} \\
$\mathrm{VShare}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}+\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}}{\sum_{\ell}(\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{\ell,t}+\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{\ell,t})}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{vshare} & $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}$ and $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ are inbound and outbound route-leg USD volumes for $k$; $\ell$ indexes every token in the day-$t$ network. \\
$\mathrm{Betweenness}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{N^{\mathrm{mid}}_{k,t}}{N^{\mathrm{route}}_t-N^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t}-N^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{betweenness\_\allowbreak{}centrality} & Fraction of day-$t$ intent routes on which $k$ is intermediate, excluding routes where $k$ is the source or sink. Superscripts identify each route role. \\
$\mathrm{Betweenness}^{\mathrm{vol}}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{mid}}_{k,t}}{\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{route}}_t-\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t}-\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{volume\_\allowbreak{}weighted\_\allowbreak{}betweenness} & USD-volume analogue of $\mathrm{Betweenness}_{k,t}$; each $\mathrm{Vol}$ term is day-$t$ route volume assigned to the indicated role. \\
$\mathrm{AllRouteVolume}_{t}$ & $\mathrm{DirectRouteVolume}_t+\mathrm{IndirectRouteVolume}_t$ & USD & \texttt{daily\_\allowbreak{}all\_\allowbreak{}route\_\allowbreak{}volume\_\allowbreak{}usd} & Total realized USD volume across direct and indirect route units on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{IndirectRouteVolume}_{t}$ & $B_t$ & USD & \texttt{daily\_\allowbreak{}indirect\_\allowbreak{}route\_\allowbreak{}volume\_\allowbreak{}usd} & $B_t$ is total realized USD volume across indirect route units on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{IndirectRouteShare}_{t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{B_t}{A_t}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{indirect\_\allowbreak{}route\_\allowbreak{}share} & $B_t$ is indirect-route USD volume and $A_t$ is all-route USD volume on day $t$. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Liquidity measures}} \\
$\mathrm{VehicleLiquidity}_{k,t}$ & $L_{k,t}=\displaystyle\sum_{p\in\mathcal L_{k,t}}\mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}$ & USD & \texttt{vehicle\_\allowbreak{}linked\_\allowbreak{}liquidity\_\allowbreak{}usd} & $\mathcal L_{k,t}$ is the set of eligible Uniswap V3 pools linked to $k$; $p$ indexes pools after filtering subgraph TVL outliers. \\
$\mathrm{LPConc}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{L_{k,t}}{\sum_{\ell\in\mathcal K}L_{\ell,t}}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{lp\_\allowbreak{}concentration} & $\mathcal K$ is the candidate-vehicle set; $L_{k,t}$ is vehicle-linked liquidity for $k$ on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{LogVehicleLiquidity}_{k,t}$ & $\ln(1+L_{k,t})$ & Natural-log points & \texttt{log\_\allowbreak{}vehicle\_\allowbreak{}linked\_\allowbreak{}liquidity} & Natural log of one plus $L_{k,t}$ measured in USD. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Route-cost opportunity measures}} \\
$\mathrm{DirectAvailable}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal D_{k,t,q}|}{|\mathcal P_{k,t,q}|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{direct\_\allowbreak{}available\_\allowbreak{}share} & Fraction of pairs in $\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ with $D_{i,j,q,t}=1$. The un-suffixed data column fixes $q=\$10{,}000$. \\
$\mathrm{VehicleAvailable}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal V_{k,t,q}|}{|\mathcal P_{k,t,q}|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{vehicle\_\allowbreak{}available\_\allowbreak{}share} & Fraction of pairs in $\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ with $V_{i,j,k,q,t}=1$, so both legs through $k$ are executable. \\
$\mathrm{NoDirectVehicleAvailable}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal V_{k,t,q}\setminus\mathcal D_{k,t,q}|}{|\mathcal P_{k,t,q}|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{no\_\allowbreak{}direct\_\allowbreak{}vehicle\_\allowbreak{}available\_\allowbreak{}share} & Fraction of pairs with $D_{i,j,q,t}=0$ and $V_{i,j,k,q,t}=1$: no executable direct route but an executable route through $k$. \\
$\mathrm{DirectDepth}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\mathrm{median}_{\mathcal D_{k,t,q}}\left(O^D_{i,j,q,t}/q\right)$ & Output USD per input USD & \texttt{direct\_\allowbreak{}depth\_\allowbreak{}median} & Median direct-route output ratio $O^D_{i,j,q,t}/q$ over executable-direct pairs $(i,j)\in\mathcal D_{k,t,q}$. \\
$\mathrm{RouteCostAdvantage}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\mathrm{median}_{\mathcal C_{k,t,q}}\Delta C_{i,j,k,q,t}$ & Basis points & \texttt{route\_\allowbreak{}cost\_\allowbreak{}advantage\_\allowbreak{}median\_\allowbreak{}bps} & Median $\Delta C_{i,j,k,q,t}$ over common-support pairs $(i,j)\in\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$; positive values favor the route through $k$. \\
$\mathrm{VehicleBeatsDirect}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal W_{k,t,q}|}{|\mathcal C_{k,t,q}|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{vehicle\_\allowbreak{}beats\_\allowbreak{}direct\_\allowbreak{}share} & Fraction of $(i,j)\in\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$ for which $\Delta C_{i,j,k,q,t}>0$, equivalently $O^V_{i,j,k,q,t}>O^D_{i,j,q,t}$. \\
$\mathrm{ThinDirectShare}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal T_{k,t,q}|}{|\mathcal P_{k,t,q}|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{thin\_\allowbreak{}direct\_\allowbreak{}share} & Fraction of pairs with $D_{i,j,q,t}=1$ and $O^D_{i,j,q,t}/q<0.9$. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Stress and dynamic variables}} \\
$\mathrm{Stress}_{t}$ & $\max\{-R^{\mathrm{WETH}}_t,0\}$ & Daily log-return fraction & \texttt{stress\_\allowbreak{}downside} & $R^{\mathrm{WETH}}_t$ is the day-$t$ WETH log return; only downside moves enter. \\
$\mathrm{StressEvent}_{t}$ & $\mathbf 1\{\mathrm{Stress}_{t}\ge 0.08\}$ & Indicator (0/1) & \texttt{stress\_\allowbreak{}event\_\allowbreak{}8pct} & Equals one when $\mathrm{Stress}_t$ is at least $0.08$ on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{FutureVehicleShare}^{(7)}_{k,t}$ & $\mathrm{VehicleShare}_{k,t+7}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{future\_\allowbreak{}bridge\_\allowbreak{}share\_\allowbreak{}t7} & Vehicle share for token $k$ seven calendar days after day $t$. \\
$\Delta_{7}\mathrm{VehicleShare}_{k,t}$ & $\mathrm{VehicleShare}_{k,t+7}-\mathrm{VehicleShare}_{k,t}$ & Fraction points & \texttt{delta\_\allowbreak{}bridge\_\allowbreak{}share\_\allowbreak{}t7} & Seven-day forward change for vehicle $k$, measured from day $t$. \\

$\mathrm{ReceiptCount}_{k,w}$ & $|\mathcal R_{k,w}|$ & Route-unit count & \texttt{settlement\_\allowbreak{}receipt\_\allowbreak{}count} & Number of receipt-audited matched route units for vehicle $k$ in UTC week $w$. \\
