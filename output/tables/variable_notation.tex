Symbol & Unit & Meaning \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Indices}} \\
$i,\ o$ & Token & Input (sold) and output (bought) endpoint tokens, respectively; $(i,o)$ is an ordered input--output pair and the direct route is $i\to o$. \\
$k$ & Token & Candidate vehicle token used as the route intermediate; $k\notin\{i,o\}$. \\
$\ell,\ p$ & Token / pool & $\ell$ indexes tokens in cross-token sums; $p$ indexes liquidity pools. \\
$t,\ w$ & UTC day / UTC week & $t$ indexes calendar days; $w$ indexes calendar weeks in settlement variables. \\
$\tau$ & Days & Positive calendar-day horizon in dynamic specifications; the empirical panels use $\tau\in\{1,7,14,30\}$. \\
$q$ & USD & Input quote notional. Un-suffixed route-cost columns use $q=\$10{,}000$. \\
$r$ & Route unit & Reconstructed input-to-output route unit. A coherent $i\to k\to o$ component contributes one $r$ regardless of its number of legs; a split or join contributes one $r$ per reconstructed input--output pair. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Route and liquidity aggregates}} \\
$\mathrm{Vol}_t,\ \mathrm{DVol}_t$ & USD & $\mathrm{Vol}_t$ is total realized USD volume across all direct and indirect route units on day $t$. $\mathrm{DVol}_t$ restricts it to direct routes, so $0\le\mathrm{DVol}_t\le\mathrm{Vol}_t$. \\
$\mathrm{IVol}_t,\ \mathrm{IVol}_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{IVol}_t$ restricts $\mathrm{Vol}_t$ to indirect routes, so $0\le\mathrm{IVol}_t\le\mathrm{Vol}_t$. $\mathrm{IVol}_{k,t}$ further restricts that volume to routes using vehicle $k$, so $0\le\mathrm{IVol}_{k,t}\le\mathrm{IVol}_t$. \\
$N_t,\ N^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t},\ N^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ & Route-unit count & $N_t$ counts all reconstructed route units $r$ on day $t$, not their individual legs. $N^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}$ and $N^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ restrict that count to routes with $k$ as the input or output endpoint, respectively; each lies between $0$ and $N_t$. \\
$N^I_t,\ N^I_{k,t}$ & Route-unit count & $N^I_t$ restricts $N_t$ to route units with at least one intermediate token. $N^I_{k,t}$ further restricts that count to units with intermediate $k$, so $0\le N^I_{k,t}\le N^I_t\le N_t$; superscript $I$ denotes indirect routes. \\
$\mathcal A_t,\ \mathcal A^k_t,\ \mathcal M^k_t$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal A_t$ is the set of endpoint pairs active in indirect routes on day $t$. $\mathcal A^k_t$ restricts it to pairs using $k$, and $\mathcal M^k_t$ further restricts it to pairs for which $k$ has the largest candidate-vehicle volume; $\mathcal M^k_t\subseteq\mathcal A^k_t\subseteq\mathcal A_t$. \\
$\mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}$ and $\mathrm{LegVol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ are route-leg USD volumes for which $k$ is the leg's input or output token, respectively, on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ & USD & Input- and output-endpoint restrictions of $\mathrm{Vol}_t$ for token $k$: $0\le\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}\le\mathrm{Vol}_t$ and $0\le\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}\le\mathrm{Vol}_t$. \\
$\mathcal K$ & Set of tokens & Prespecified candidate set $\{\mathrm{WETH},\mathrm{USDC},\mathrm{USDT},\mathrm{DAI},\mathrm{WBTC}\}$. \\
$\mathcal L_{k,t},\ m_p$ & Set of pools / token count & $\mathcal L_{k,t}$ contains Uniswap V3 daily-snapshot pools with candidate $k$ on one side, exact token contracts identified in the persisted V3 swap archive, and $0<\mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}\le 10$ billion USD. $m_p$ is the number of pool tokens in $\mathcal K$, so $m_p\in\{1,2\}$. \\
$\mathrm{TVL}_{p,t},\ L_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}$ is pool $p$'s day-$t$ USD TVL from the V3 daily snapshot. $L_{k,t}=\sum_{p\in\mathcal L_{k,t}}\mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}/m_p$; a one-candidate pool contributes all TVL to that candidate and a two-candidate pool contributes one half to each. \\
$R^{\mathrm{WETH}}_t$ & Daily log-return fraction & Day-$t$ log return of the WETH price used to construct downside stress. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Route-cost quote objects}} \\
$\mathcal{P}_{k,t,q}$ & Set of token pairs & Quote-universe pairs for candidate $k$: distinct ordered input--output pairs among day $t$'s 200 largest clean reconstructed pairs by realized USD volume, where clean means route class \texttt{single} or \texttt{coherent}, $k\notin\{i,o\}$, and each of $i$, $o$, and $k$ has a valid day-price estimate. A valid estimate requires at least three finite token-side USD-per-token observations in $(0,\$1{,}000{,}000)$ and equals their realized-USD-volume-weighted median. Each pair is submitted to the direct and via-$k$ quote engines at input $q$; membership does not require either quote to execute. \\
$\mathcal{D}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{I}_{k,t,q}$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal D_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ and $\mathcal I_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ restrict the broad pair set to pairs with an executable direct route or an executable indirect route via $k$. \\
$\mathcal{C}_{k,t,q}$ & Set of token pairs & Common-support pairs for candidate $k$, day $t$, and notional $q$: $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}=\mathcal D_{k,t,q}\cap\mathcal I_{k,t,q}$, so both the direct and indirect routes are executable. \\
$\mathcal{T}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{W}_{k,t,q}$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal T_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal D_{k,t,q}$ is the thin-direct subset. $\mathcal W_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$ contains common-support pairs for which the indirect route beats the direct route, equivalently $\Delta C^D_{i,o,k,q,t}<0$. \\
$D_{i,o,q,t},\ I_{i,o,k,q,t},\ T_{i,o,q,t}$ & Indicator (0/1) & Indicators for direct-route availability ($D$), indirect-route-through-$k$ availability ($I$), and an executable direct route returning less than $0.9q$ ($T$). \\
$O^{D}_{i,o,q,t},\ O^{I}_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & USD & Quoted output values; superscripts $D$ and $I$ denote direct and indirect routes. \\
$\Delta C^D_{i,o,k,q,t}$ & Fraction & Pair-level direct cost advantage $(O^{D}_{i,o,q,t}-O^{I}_{i,o,k,q,t})/O^{D}_{i,o,q,t}$ on $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$; positive values favor the direct route. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Settlement objects and operators}} \\
$\mathcal{R}_{k,w}$ & Set of route units & Receipt-audited matched route units using vehicle $k$ in UTC week $w$. \\
$\mathcal{R}^{\mathrm{transfer}}_{k,w}$ & Set of route units & Members whose receipt logs a transfer of vehicle $k$; $\mathcal R^{\mathrm{transfer}}_{k,w}\subseteq\mathcal R_{k,w}$. \\
$|\mathcal{S}|,\ \mathbf{1}_{\{\cdot\}}$ & Count / indicator (0/1) & Cardinality of any finite set $\mathcal S$ and an indicator equal to one when its subscripted condition is true. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Dynamic operators}} \\
$\Delta_{\tau}$ &  & Change in a daily variable over the $\tau$ days ending at $t$: $\Delta_\tau X_t=X_t-X_{t-\tau}$. \\
