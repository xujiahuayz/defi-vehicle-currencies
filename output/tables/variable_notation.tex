Symbol & Unit & Meaning \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Indices}} \\
$i,\ j$ & Token & Input (sold) and output (bought) endpoint tokens; the ordered pair is $i\to j$. \\
$k$ & Token & Candidate vehicle token used as the route intermediate; $k\notin\{i,j\}$. \\
$\ell,\ p$ & Token / pool & $\ell$ indexes tokens in cross-token sums; $p$ indexes liquidity pools. \\
$t,\ w$ & UTC day / UTC week & $t$ indexes calendar days; $w$ indexes calendar weeks in settlement variables. \\
$q$ & USD & Input quote notional. Un-suffixed route-cost columns use $q=\$10{,}000$. \\
$r$ & Route unit & Receipt-audited coherent multihop route unit indexed by $r$. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Route and liquidity aggregates}} \\
$A_t,\ \mathrm{DVol}_t$ & USD & $A_t$ is total realized USD volume across all direct and indirect route units on day $t$. $\mathrm{DVol}_t$ restricts it to direct routes, so $0\le\mathrm{DVol}_t\le A_t$. \\
$\mathrm{IVol}_t,\ \mathrm{IVol}_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{IVol}_t$ restricts $A_t$ to indirect routes, so $0\le\mathrm{IVol}_t\le A_t$. $\mathrm{IVol}_{k,t}$ further restricts that volume to routes using vehicle $k$, so $0\le\mathrm{IVol}_{k,t}\le\mathrm{IVol}_t$. \\
$N^{\mathrm{route}}_t,\ N^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t},\ N^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}$ & Route count & $N^{\mathrm{route}}_t$ counts all day-$t$ intent routes. $N^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t}$ and $N^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}$ restrict that count to source and sink roles for $k$; each lies between $0$ and $N^{\mathrm{route}}_t$. \\
$N^B_t,\ N^B_{k,t}$ & Route-unit count & $N^B_t$ restricts $N^{\mathrm{route}}_t$ to indirect routes. $N^B_{k,t}$ further restricts that count to routes using $k$, so $0\le N^B_{k,t}\le N^B_t\le N^{\mathrm{route}}_t$; superscript $B$ denotes bridged (indirect) routes. \\
$\mathcal A_t,\ \mathcal A^k_t,\ \mathcal M^k_t$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal A_t$ is the set of endpoint pairs active in indirect routes on day $t$. $\mathcal A^k_t$ restricts it to pairs using $k$, and $\mathcal M^k_t$ further restricts it to pairs for which $k$ has the largest candidate-vehicle volume; $\mathcal M^k_t\subseteq\mathcal A^k_t\subseteq\mathcal A_t$. \\
$\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ & USD & $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{in}}_{k,t}$ and $\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{out}}_{k,t}$ are inbound and outbound route-leg volumes assigned to token $k$ on day $t$. \\
$\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t},\ \mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}$ & USD & Source- and sink-role restrictions of $A_t$ for token $k$: $0\le\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t}\le A_t$ and $0\le\mathrm{Vol}^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}\le A_t$. \\
$\mathcal K,\ L_{k,t}$ & Set of tokens / USD & $\mathcal K$ is the candidate-vehicle set. $L_{k,t}$ is liquidity linked to candidate $k\in\mathcal K$ on day $t$. \\
$\mathcal L_{k,t},\ \mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}$ & Set of pools / USD & $\mathcal L_{k,t}$ is the set of eligible pools linked to $k$ on day $t$; $\mathrm{TVL}_{p,t}$ is total value locked in pool $p$ on that day. \\
$R^{\mathrm{WETH}}_t$ & Daily log-return fraction & Day-$t$ log return of the WETH price used to construct downside stress. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Route-cost quote objects}} \\
$\mathcal{P}_{k,t,q}$ & Set of token pairs & Ordered endpoint pairs eligible for $k$ and quoted on day $t$ at notional $q$. \\
$\mathcal{D}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{V}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{C}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{T}_{k,t,q},\ \mathcal{W}_{k,t,q}$ & Sets of token pairs & $\mathcal D_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ and $\mathcal V_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ restrict the broad pair set to pairs with a direct route or a route via $k$. $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}=\mathcal D_{k,t,q}\cap\mathcal V_{k,t,q}$, $\mathcal T_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal D_{k,t,q}$ is thin-direct support, and $\mathcal W_{k,t,q}\subseteq\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$ has a vehicle-route cost advantage. \\
$D_{i,j,q,t},\ V_{i,j,k,q,t},\ T_{i,j,q,t}$ & Indicator (0/1) & Indicators for direct-route availability ($D$), route-through-$k$ availability ($V$), and an executable direct route returning less than $0.9q$ ($T$). \\
$O^{D}_{i,j,q,t},\ O^{V}_{i,j,k,q,t}$ & USD & Quoted output values; superscripts $D$ and $V$ denote direct and vehicle routes. \\
$\Delta C_{i,j,k,q,t}$ & Basis points & $10{,}000\,(O^{V}_{i,j,k,q,t}-O^{D}_{i,j,q,t})/O^{D}_{i,j,q,t}$ on $\mathcal C_{k,t,q}$. \\
\addlinespace
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Settlement objects and operators}} \\
$\mathcal{R}_{k,w}$ & Set of route units & Receipt-audited matched route units using vehicle $k$ in UTC week $w$. \\
$\mathcal{R}^{\mathrm{transfer}}_{k,w}$ & Set of route units & Members whose receipt logs a transfer of vehicle $k$; $\mathcal R^{\mathrm{transfer}}_{k,w}\subseteq\mathcal R_{k,w}$. \\
$|\mathcal{S}|,\ \mathbf{1}_{\{\cdot\}}$ & Count / indicator (0/1) & Cardinality of any finite set $\mathcal S$ and an indicator equal to one when its subscripted condition is true. \\
$\Delta_{7}$ & 7 days & Seven-day forward change in any daily variable $X_t$: $\Delta_7 X_t=X_{t+7}-X_t$. \\

$\mathrm{Betweenness}_{k,t}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{N^{B}_{k,t}}{N^{\mathrm{route}}_t-N^{\mathrm{src}}_{k,t}-N^{\mathrm{sink}}_{k,t}}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{betweenness\_\allowbreak{}centrality} & Fraction of day-$t$ intent routes on which $k$ is intermediate, excluding routes where $k$ is the source or sink. Superscripts identify each route role. \\

$A_t$ & $\mathrm{DVol}_t+\mathrm{IVol}_t$ & USD & \texttt{daily\_\allowbreak{}all\_\allowbreak{}route\_\allowbreak{}volume\_\allowbreak{}usd} & Total realized USD volume across direct and indirect route units on day $t$. \\

$\mathrm{LogVehicleLiquidity}_{k,t}$ & $\ln(1+L_{k,t})$ & Natural-log points & \texttt{log\_\allowbreak{}vehicle\_\allowbreak{}linked\_\allowbreak{}liquidity} & Natural log of one plus $L_{k,t}$ measured in USD. \\

\multicolumn{5}{@{}l}{\textit{Route-cost opportunity measures}} \\
$\mathrm{DirectAvailable}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal D_{k,t,q}|}{|\mathcal P_{k,t,q}|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{direct\_\allowbreak{}available\_\allowbreak{}share} & Fraction of pairs in $\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ with $D_{i,j,q,t}=1$. $\mathrm{VehicleAvailable}_{k,t,q}$ & $\displaystyle\frac{|\mathcal V_{k,t,q}|}{|\mathcal P_{k,t,q}|}$ & Fraction (0--1) & \texttt{vehicle\_\allowbreak{}available\_\allowbreak{}share} & Fraction of pairs in $\mathcal P_{k,t,q}$ with $V_{i,j,k,q,t}=1$, so both legs through $k$ are executable. \\
