# Core Empirical RQ Results

Generated by `scripts/run_core_rq_experiments.py`. Core notes only, not manuscript prose.

## Research Questions And Answers

| RQ | Empirical answer | Exact evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RQ1. Formation | Vehicle use is higher when the candidate vehicle expands executable route opportunity, improves route quality within common-support cells, and has larger vehicle-linked liquidity. | actual_route_choice: route-cost advantage is positive for actual vehicle share (beta 0.1462, t 27.02, p <0.001); vehicle availability is positive (beta 19.1385, t 39.58, p <0.001); vehicle depth is positive (beta 28.8502, t 49.92, p <0.001). core_panel_regressions: route-cost advantage predicts future VehicleShare (beta 0.0001, t 3.10, p 0.002); vehicle availability predicts future VehicleShare (beta 0.0940, t 6.67, p <0.001). |
| RQ2. Liquidity provision | Vehicle-linked liquidity and vehicle use reinforce each other in stock allocations; short-run stock changes also load on route availability. | core_panel_regressions/lp_allocation_feedback: LPConcentration predicts future VehicleShare (beta 0.1117, t 8.30, p <0.001); VehicleShare predicts future LPConcentration (beta 0.0419, t 4.29, p <0.001) and future log VehicleLinkedLiquidity (beta 1.1713, t 18.86, p <0.001). lp_allocation_feedback: vehicle availability predicts the 30-day change in log VehicleLinkedLiquidity (beta 0.2112, t 4.65, p <0.001). |
| RQ3. Persistence and displacement | Vehicle status is persistent, but challenger cost edges predict actual challenger share gains and incumbent losses. | core_panel_regressions: current VehicleShare predicts t+30 VehicleShare (beta 0.3630, t 21.98, p <0.001). persistence_thresholds: >250 bp challenger edge implies incumbent share change -10.08 pp, p <0.001. pair_challenger_displacement: challenger edge raises challenger share change (beta 0.0501, t 14.54, p <0.001) and lowers incumbent share change (beta -0.0408, t -10.30, p <0.001). |
| RQ4. Stress rotation | Stress rotates vehicle use away from WETH and toward stable vehicles on common-support stress days; event-time persistence is interpreted with pre-movement caution. | p3_stress_rotation: WETH-minus-stable change is -2.96 pp, p 0.018. stress_event_time: event-day gap is -26.96 pp, p 0.006; pre-window gap is -5.63 pp, p 0.016. |
| RQ5. Architecture | Uniswap V3 materially deepens direct-route availability and reduces no-direct WETH dependence, especially outside already-strong direct markets. | p4a_v3_opportunity: no-direct WETH availability falls -25.81 pp, p <0.001. v3_dose_response: in Q2 pre-V3 direct markets, direct-route availability rises 51.36 pp, p <0.001; no-direct WETH availability falls -47.51 pp, p <0.001. |
| RQ6. Settlement design | V4 reduces physical intermediate-token transfers while vehicle-route demand persists across matched endpoint-vehicle-week cells. | p4b_v4_settlement: V4 transfer incidence is 81.4% versus V3 100.0%; matched-cell route-size balance is log route diff=-0.692. v4_route_use_persistence: log V3 route count predicts log V4 route count (beta 0.3674, t 28.33, p <0.001); log V3 route volume predicts log V4 route volume (beta 0.5819, t 83.13, p <0.001). |
| RQ7. Common liquidity | Vehicle-linked pools share a vehicle-specific liquidity component beyond market-wide liquidity; the component is stronger in high-vehicle-dependence samples and survives top-pool exclusion. | common_liquidity: vehicle factor is positive in the full sample (beta 0.1626, t 3.77, p <0.001); stress interaction is positive (beta 0.1064, t 2.17, p 0.030). common_liquidity_heterogeneity: high-dependence vehicle factor is positive (beta 0.2174, t 4.69, p <0.001); low-dependence vehicle factor is not significant (beta -0.0921, t -1.02, p 0.307); excluding top 1% mean-liquidity pools remains positive (beta 0.1670, t 3.83, p <0.001). |
