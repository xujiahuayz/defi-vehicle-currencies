## D2 route-authority rebinding — DONE, published 2026-08-13 (`dc8b074`)

The blocker was real and is closed.

**Diagnosis.** The all-day relocation dry-run halted at `fluid/swaps/20241030`
because the raw **column projection / query contract** had drifted, not the data.
A Dune refetch at 2026-08-13T00:25Z overwrote exactly 70 Fluid days,
2024-10-30..2025-01-07, that had been symlinks into the certified sibling store.
The refetch wrote the **wide 24-column** payload plus a `streams{}`-shaped
sidecar carrying `query_contract_sha256`, `query_start_date` and
`query_end_date_exclusive`; the certified generation is the **lean 12-column**
payload with a flat sidecar carrying none of those. Five identity fields moved:
`logical_content_sha256`, `metadata_sha256`, `observed_query_contract_sha256`,
`observed_query_start_date`, `observed_query_end_date_exclusive`.

**Scope, measured not assumed.** Scanning all 610 Fluid partitions against
`data/manifests/route_release_pre_relocation_authority.json`: exactly 70 days
differ, all in that contiguous range, all with the same five fields. The 29
2026-06 Fluid days were **already identical** to the snapshot, so the earlier
plan to re-project them was unnecessary and would have broken the gate. No other
venue drifted: the full 12,802-partition dry-run passes.

**Repair, no provider acquisition.** Restored all 70 days byte-exact from
`../defi-dominant-currency/data/fluid/{raw_swaps,raw_meta}/`, verified before
writing that those bytes reproduce the snapshot's `logical_content_sha256` and
`metadata_sha256` for all 70 days, installed atomically and hardlinked into both
worktrees. Then re-ran the Fluid local scan and certificate (610/610 passed,
certificate `690ab0f3…`), then the complete dry-run (2,332 days, all
authority-equivalent, 2,332 fingerprints changed = the relocation itself), then
`--publish`. `require_route_release()` passes. Tests
`test_raw_certification.py` + `test_route_marker_migration.py`: 79 passed.

Re-running the dry-run against the same snapshot now fails with "route release
marker or ledger changed after authority snapshot" — that is the gate working
after publication, not a regression.
