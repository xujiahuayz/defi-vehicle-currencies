\begin{frame}{Stablecoin dominance grew through changes in routed markets}
\begin{itemize}
\item Stablecoins lead routed value in 2022 and again in 2025--2026, but fall behind native assets in 2023--2024.
\vspace{0.30em}
\item USDT drives the post-2024 rise in excess stablecoin intermediation; USDC changes little.
\vspace{0.30em}
\item The rise comes mainly from the pairs available for indirect trade, activity across those pairs, and whether they use an intermediary; substitution within continuing vehicle-using pairs is small.
\end{itemize}
\vfill
\begin{beamercolorbox}[sep=0.24cm,wd=\textwidth]{block body}
\centering
\textbf{Vehicle dominance determines which pools carry indirect order flow. The rotation toward stablecoins therefore shifts the trading and liquidity demand generated by exchanges between other assets.}
\end{beamercolorbox}
\end{frame}
